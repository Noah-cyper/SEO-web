## The short answer

Mitsubishi Electric is **phasing out the FX3 series (FX3U / FX3UC / FX3G)**. The successor is the **iQ-F / FX5 series** — with the **FX5U** as the main replacement, now joined by FX5UJ and FX5S to cover the full former FX3 range.

Two things engineers get wrong about this transition:

1. **The FX5U is *not* a drop-in.** Wiring and dimensions differ from the FX3U, and programs must be migrated into **GX Works3** — with a review pass to confirm every function carries over.
2. **You don't have to panic-replace working FX3U machines.** Genuine FX3U stock still exists in distribution, but it shrinks every year — and the counterfeit rate on Mitsubishi PLCs is high. Spec new panels on FX5U; keep running FX3U assets supplied safely.

## Should you migrate now or keep the FX3U running?

**Migrate to FX5U when:**
- You're building a **new panel**, doing a **retrofit**, or **expanding capacity**.
- You want the longest support runway and modern connectivity (Ethernet, positioning).
- You're standardizing a fleet and want one platform going forward.

**Keep the FX3U (and source spares) when:**
- The machine works and there's no engineering budget to re-wire/re-program right now.
- You need to protect a production line until a planned upgrade window.
- You just need a genuine spare CPU/module to cover a failure.

Both are valid. The wrong move is being *forced* into an emergency migration because a spare failed and no genuine FX3U could be found in time.

## FAQ

**Is the Mitsubishi FX3U discontinued?**
It is being phased out. Mitsubishi Electric has begun end-of-life for the FX3 series and positions the iQ-F / FX5 series as the successor. Genuine FX3U stock is still available in distribution but shrinking.

**What replaces the Mitsubishi FX3U?**
The FX5U (iQ-F series) is the main replacement, with FX5UJ and FX5S covering the rest of the former FX3 range.

**Is the FX5U a drop-in replacement for the FX3U?**
No. Wiring and dimensions differ, and programs must be migrated into GX Works3 with a review to confirm all functions transfer correctly.

**Can I still buy a genuine FX3U?**
Yes, from remaining distribution stock — but because the FX3U is heavily counterfeited, only buy from a source that provides functional testing and traceability.

**Do I have to replace working FX3U machines now?**
No. There's no need to panic-replace working hardware. Specify FX5U for new/retrofit work and keep existing FX3U lines supplied with genuine, screened spares.
