## Your line is down. The clock is the cost.

When a critical part fails and production stops, every hour has a number attached to it. You don't need a catalog — you need the **right genuine part, found fast, and confirmed real** before it goes on your machine. That's what this service is for: **emergency, line-down and AOG-style sourcing** of industrial automation and instrumentation parts.

**→ [Send the part number now](/en/sourcing/) — we prioritize line-down requests.**

---

## What "emergency supply" means here

- **Speed with proof.** Fast doesn't mean skipping verification — we screen for counterfeits even under time pressure, because a fake part that fails again turns one outage into two.
- **Hard-to-find under pressure.** The parts that fail at the worst time are often the obsolete or long-lead ones. Finding those quickly is exactly our specialty.
- **One call, one owner.** You talk to one accountable partner who drives the whole search, verification and shipment — not a queue of resellers.

---

## What to send us (so we move fast)

To quote and locate in the shortest time, send:

1. **Part / model number** and, if possible, serial and range.
2. **Certification** required (SIL, Ex/ATEX, etc.).
3. **Quantity** and delivery location.
4. **How critical** — is the line stopped now, or is this a protective spare?
5. A **photo of the tag** if you're unsure of the exact number.

A tag photo alone is enough to start. We cross-reference the rest.

---

## How we handle a line-down request

1. **Prioritized intake** — line-down requests jump the queue.
2. **Parallel search** — we hit the multi-country approved network at once, including obsolete and reconditioned sources.
3. **Authenticity check** — counterfeit screening and functional test, so what ships actually works.
4. **Fastest viable option first** — we lead with whatever restores production soonest, then present alternatives (genuine stock, reconditioned, or a qualified drop-in / successor).
5. **Traceable shipment** — documentation follows the part.

---

## Why this matters for critical assets

For safety-instrumented and hazardous-area points, "any part that fits" is not acceptable — the replacement has to carry the **right certification and be genuine**. Our layered inspection and Approved Vendor List exist precisely so that a rushed replacement doesn't become a safety or compliance problem later.

## FAQ

**How fast can you supply an emergency part?**
It depends on the part and its location in our network, but line-down requests are prioritized and searched in parallel. Send the part number and we'll give you the fastest realistic option with a time.

**Do you verify parts even for urgent orders?**
Yes. We screen for counterfeits and functionally test even under time pressure — a fake part that fails again costs more than the wait.

**What information do you need to start?**
Part/model number (or a photo of the tag), certification, quantity, delivery location, and how critical it is.

**Can you handle obsolete parts in an emergency?**
Yes — obsolete and long-lead parts are exactly the ones that fail at the worst time, and finding them fast is our specialty.
