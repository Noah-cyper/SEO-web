## The short answer

The **Rosemount 1151 pressure transmitter** — the process-industry standard for more than 40 years — is now **obsolete (discontinued by Emerson)**. If a 1151 fails on your line today, you have three realistic paths:

1. **Upgrade to the Rosemount 3051** — the factory-designated successor. Better accuracy and stability, but not a mechanical drop-in.
2. **Fit a drop-in replacement** — e.g. the Curtiss-Wright **DTC3**, engineered as a form-fit-function replacement for the 1151.
3. **Source a reconditioned / remaining-stock 1151** — to keep a like-for-like unit running until a planned upgrade.

Which one is right depends on your downtime tolerance, whether you can touch the wiring/mounting, and how many units you have to standardize. Below is how to decide — and how we source each option with full traceability and counterfeit screening.

## Why the 1151 is being phased out

Emerson has moved the 1151 to end-of-life and steers users toward the newer 3051 platform. The practical problem for plants — especially **aging power, nuclear, refining and petrochemical assets** built around the 1151 — is that genuine new stock has dried up, calibration/spares support is winding down, and the **counterfeit and grey-market risk climbs** every year for a part this widely installed.

That is exactly the gap a sourcing partner fills: keeping legacy assets alive safely while you plan a migration on your own schedule — not the vendor's.

## FAQ

**Is the Rosemount 1151 discontinued?**
Yes. Emerson has moved the 1151 to obsolete/end-of-life status and directs users to the newer 3051 platform.

**What is the direct replacement for the Rosemount 1151?**
The factory successor is the Rosemount 3051. For a same-footprint swap, purpose-built drop-ins such as the Curtiss-Wright DTC3 are engineered to replace the 1151 directly.

**Can I still buy a Rosemount 1151?**
New OEM production has ended, but reconditioned and remaining-stock units are still available through specialist suppliers. Because the 1151 is heavily counterfeited, buy only from a source that provides testing and traceability.

**Is the 3051 a drop-in for the 1151?**
No — plan for differences in mounting and wiring. If you need a true drop-in, use a form-fit-function replacement instead.

**Can you match my exact 1151 range and certification?**
Yes. Send the model/serial from the tag and we'll cross-reference the correct replacement, including SIL and hazardous-area (Ex/ATEX) option codes.
