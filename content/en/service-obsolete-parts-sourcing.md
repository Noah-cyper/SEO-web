## What we source

- **Obsolete & discontinued components** — instrumentation, PLCs, drives, I/O, sensors and more that the OEM no longer produces.
- **End-of-life (EOL) & last-time-buy (LTB)** — we secure remaining genuine stock before it disappears, and flag qualified alternatives where a like-for-like no longer exists.
- **Hard-to-find & long-lead parts** — items nobody seems to have, located through our network instead of a single catalog.
- **Qualified replacements & cross-references** — when the original is truly gone, we recommend a verified equivalent or the OEM-designated successor.

---

## How the sourcing process works

1. **You send the details** — part/model number, serial, range, certification (SIL, Ex/ATEX), and how urgent it is. A photo of the tag is enough to start.
2. **We search the approved network** — multi-country suppliers on our Approved Vendor List, each scored and monitored on quality, delivery and authenticity.
3. **We verify authenticity** — layered inspection and functional testing to screen out counterfeit and substandard parts.
4. **You get options** — genuine remaining stock, a reconditioned unit, or a qualified replacement / OEM successor — each with lead time, price and documentation.
5. **We ship with traceability** — full documentation you can hand to QA and audit.

## FAQ

**Can you find parts that are completely obsolete?**
Often, yes — through remaining genuine stock, reconditioned units, or a qualified replacement. Send the part number and we'll tell you what's realistic, with lead time.

**How do you make sure a part isn't counterfeit?**
Every supplier is on our Approved Vendor List, and every part goes through a layered inspection and functional-test program before it ships, with traceability documentation.

**What if there's no equivalent left at all?**
We recommend the OEM-designated successor or a qualified cross-reference, and help you plan the change (wiring, footprint, certification).

**How fast can you supply?**
It depends on the part, but we handle emergency and line-down cases specifically — see [Emergency / line-down supply](/en/services/emergency-line-down-supply/).
